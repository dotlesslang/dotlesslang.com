What's it all about?

The Arabic alphabet as we know it, although familiar, is not the original. The ancient Arabs had no need for diacritic marks or dots to decipher the meanings of words, but later generations did…

...until now. The Dotless Arabic keyboard leverages machine learning technology to accurately predict words based on their context, eliminating 12 keys from the standard Arabic keyboard to provide a simpler and easier typing experience.

Our Goal

⌨️

Simplified Keyboard

Dotless reduces the number of keys by using machine learning to differentiate between similar letters (e.g., ج، ح، خ) based on context, drastically simplifying the layout.

🧠

N-Gram ML Model

We use a statistical n-gram model trained on a large corpus of Arabic sentences to make accurate predictions. The iOS app is currently in alpha testing.

🌍

Multi-Language Support

We plan to extend support to other languages written in the Arabic script — Farsi, Urdu, Kurdish, Uyghur, and many others, In Shaa Allah!
