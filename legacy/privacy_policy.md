### Privacy Policy

- The publishers and developers of the Dotless keyboard app do not collect or receive any of your personal data.
- In order to enhance the user experience and ensure better predictions, Dotless may store some information about your typing habits, such as adding new words to the dictionary or information about commonly used words.
- However, all such data is stored locally on files in your phone and is not sent to any third parties, nor is it transmitted by the app across any network.
